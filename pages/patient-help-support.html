General Questions
How do I book an appointment?

Go to the Book Appointment page from the patient menu. Select your preferred clinic service, doctor, date and time. Complete the required information and submit your appointment request.

Where can I check my appointment status?

Open the My Appointments page to view your upcoming appointments and their latest status, such as Pending, Approved or Rejected.

Where can I view my previous appointments?

Visit the Appointment History page to review your previous appointment records.

Can I update my personal information?

Yes. Open the My Profile page to view and update the personal information available in your patient account.

Where can I find information about clinic services?

Select Clinic Services from the patient menu to view the services currently available at Healora Clinic.

What should I do if I experience a problem with my account?

You can submit a support request using the form below. Select the relevant enquiry category and describe the issue clearly so the clinic team can understand your request.
